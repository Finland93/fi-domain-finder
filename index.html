About rate limits — read this first. Public, free RDAP endpoints throttle you. rdap.org sits behind Cloudflare and allows roughly 10 requests per 10 seconds; go faster and you get HTTP 429 (rate limited). A red "unsure" cell means the check failed — usually a rate limit — it does not mean the domain is taken or free. This tool protects you automatically: it keeps requests serial by default, backs off and slows down when it sees 429s, and re-queues throttled cells for a second pass. Practical tips: keep the delay at 1100 ms or higher, leave concurrency at 1 for rdap.org, scan in smaller batches using the filters, and press "Retry unsure" after a run to clean up leftovers.

Where the free ones hide. Pure two-letter combos (aa–zz) are essentially all registered. Your best odds are combos with digits or the å/ä/ö letters, and of course the much larger 3-character space.

Heads up on 3 characters: that is 59,319 combinations. At a safe ~1 request/second a full scan takes many hours — the timer shows a live estimate. Use the filters to scan a slice at a time (e.g. only-digits or only-å/ä/ö) instead of the whole set at once.

Confirm and register a free name via the official search at registry.domain.fi. Registration always goes through an accredited registrar (Zoner, Louhi, Domainhotelli, etc.). Note: some short names are premium-priced.
